## 10 observações que fiz (não resumo — o que me chamou atenção)
1. The trade-off of self-hosting N8N is that it doens't give pre-authorized OAuth for Google / Instagram Service. I decided to use the Cloud version so I don't have to manage each OAuth client for every provider individually.
2. Before an agent works on an Objective like "Grow instagram account in 10% of users" it is needed to have clear metrics and a feedback loop tested. An objective without a measurement loop ins't an agent optmizing anything. 
3. An autonomous agent will think and decide for itself. In an instagram account the risk is what it will be published. How to validate if there's no offense, the tone is right, it's not full of prejudice, and other implications we may have in communication. Testing and validating it before it grows it what is going to help with the right guardrails for this agent.
